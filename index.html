Who is PinkPantheress?

Victoria Walker, better known as PinkPantheress, is a 25 year old British pop artist. Her sound is a combo of alt-pop, UK garage, R&B, DnB, and much more!

How it started ─ · · 𝄞

After anonymously uploading snippets of her self-produced tracks on TikTok in 2020, PinkPantheress' work spread across the internet and gained attention. With songs like Pain & Just a Waste, it didn't take long for the artist to blow up.
